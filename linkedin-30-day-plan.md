## 📋 Strategy (read this first)

**Why LinkedIn:** For B2B web development, LinkedIn is where founders, marketing managers, and ops leads hang out. A strong presence here (a) drives direct inbound leads, (b) drives Google searches for "Sky Web Innovations," and (c) builds the brand trust that makes every other off-page signal stronger.

**Your content pillars (rotate through these):**
1. **Educate** — teach something about web dev, SaaS, SEO (builds authority)
2. **Proof** — projects, results, behind-the-scenes (builds trust)
3. **Perspective** — opinions, myths, hot takes (builds reach/engagement)
4. **Invite** — soft CTAs to start a conversation (drives leads)

**Formatting rules for reach:**
- **Hook in the first 2 lines** (before the "see more" fold) — this decides everything
- **One idea per post** — don't cram
- **Short lines + white space** — mobile-friendly
- **End with a question or soft CTA** — comments boost distribution
- **3-5 hashtags max**, placed at the bottom (e.g. #WebDevelopment #SaaS #Laravel)
- **Reply to every comment within 60 min** — engagement in the first hour is huge
- **No external links in the post body** (LinkedIn suppresses them) — put links in the first comment if needed
